# Poster brief

- **Purpose:** conference/course-fair poster summarising the SPORTS AI prototype and its HCI evaluation framework.
- **Audience:** HCI/AI students and faculty; non-experts passing by.
- **Format:** A1 portrait (594×841 mm), 3-column grid, readable from 1.5 m; QR to repo/demo placeholder `[QR]`.
- **One message:** *Show the reason, not just the number – and measure whether it helps.*
- **Honesty rules:** every ML number is a *synthetic benchmark result*; the HCI study is *proposed*, with **no human-participant results yet**. No claims of real-world accuracy or ethics approval.
- **Sections:** Title · Problem · System (architecture figure) · How the models were built (pipeline figure) · Synthetic benchmark table · Explainability + what-if screenshot · HCI design (conditions + flow) · Hypotheses (untested) · Limitations · Next steps · References (verified subset) · Contact placeholders.
- **Assets:** `research/poster_assets/` (figures, tables, screenshots).
- **Style:** calm light background, one accent colour per sport (cricket green, football blue, basketball orange) used consistently; sans-serif (Inter); figures large, text short.
